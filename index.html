Long Beach City College serves as the lead institution for the LA-25 LARC and LBCC AI Literacy and Innovation Project.

This regional convening brings together faculty, administrators, and institutional leaders from community colleges across the Los Angeles region to explore AI literacy, innovation, curriculum integration, and equitable implementation strategies in higher education.

The LA-25 LARC and LBCC AI Literacy & Innovation Project is our regional answer — and Long Beach City College is honored to serve as the lead institution. This virtual session re-runs the kick-off for colleagues who couldn't join us in person on May 22 — the same welcome, orientation, and first contact with the cohort you will spend the months ahead building with.

05Common Questions

Before you join.

If your question isn't here, reach out to Ruth — she'll get you what you need.

How do I get the Zoom link?

Register for the session and your personal join link will be emailed to you ahead of June 12. If you haven't received it by the day before, reach out to Ruth Amanuel using the contact details below. Plan to join about 10 minutes early.

I attended the in-person kick-off on May 22 — should I join this too?

You're welcome to, but it isn't necessary. This online session re-runs the same kick-off content for colleagues who couldn't be there in person. If you've already attended, you're all set — though you're always welcome to drop in.

Who should attend?

The session is designed for enrolled track participants and institutional leadership — presidents, VPIs, deans of career education, and CTE coordinators — especially anyone who couldn't make the in-person convening. Each partner college is welcome to send leadership in addition to track faculty.

Will the session be recorded?

Yes. The session will be recorded and posted to the regional Canvas course so colleagues who can't attend live can catch up. If you register, we'll make sure you receive the recording and all materials.

What do I need to take part?

A computer or tablet, a stable internet connection, and the Zoom app or a current web browser. All materials — track guides and the Canvas course — will be available online.

Do I need my camera on?

No — your camera is entirely optional. Join however you're most comfortable. Live captions will be available in Zoom throughout.

How do I get access to the Canvas course?

The regional Canvas course is reserved for accepted program participants only — faculty who completed the signup form and were formally recruited and accepted into the program by their sector pathway lead.

If that's you, no action is needed. The lead college (Long Beach City College) will onboard you onto Canvas automatically using the email you provided on your signup form. You'll receive your Canvas invitation in the days following the session. Once enrolled, you can open the regional Canvas course directly.

If you are an accepted participant and have not received Canvas access, please contact Ruth Amanuel at ramanuel@lbcc.edu first — she'll verify your enrollment and resolve it directly. Accepted participants who need a manual fallback may self-enroll here.
