01About the Convening

A regional answer to a question that won't wait.

What does it mean to teach, learn, and lead in a world reshaped by AI?
18
Community Colleges
4

LA Region

This regional convening brings together faculty, administrators, and institutional leaders from community colleges across the Los Angeles region to explore AI literacy, innovation, curriculum integration, and equitable implementation strategies in higher education.

The LA-25 LARC and LBCC AI Literacy & Innovation Project is our regional answer — and Long Beach City College is honored to serve as the lead institution. The Kick-Off Convening is where the project goes from architecture to motion: welcome, orientation, and first contact with the cohort you will spend the months ahead building with.

02Run of Show

The day, arc by arc.

Four and a half hours organized around three priorities: inspire and build buy-in, build cross-college community, then deliver foundational content and activate.

i. Morning · Inspire & Build Buy-In
Leadership voices, vision, and a live faculty proof-of-concept

Doors open at 9:30 AM for coffee, registration, and informal networking. The formal convening begins at 10:00 AM.

  • Welcome & Run of Show from project leadership
  • LBCC Leadership Remarks — institutional welcome from the host campus
  • Regional Welcome from the Los Angeles Regional Consortium
  • Recognition of Participating Institutions and peer-college voice
  • Group Photo (timing TBD)
  • Vision Moment — "Why Now, Why Us"
  • AI Landscape Briefing — external expert grounding on AI's impact across LA workforce sectors
  • Faculty Proof-of-Concept — a live AI integration demo from a real classroom
ii. Late Morning · Build Cross-College Community
Faculty meet faculty across institutional lines
  • Cross-College Mixer — facilitated same-track, different-college pairings
  • Networking Lunch with track-based seating and conversation prompts
iii. Afternoon · Deliver Content & Activate
Track snapshots, logistics, and cross-track collaboration
  • Four Track Snapshots — a walk through each track's journey
  • Project Logistics & Canvas Tour — calendar, support infrastructure, and a brief Canvas shell tour
  • Sector Pathway Cross-Pollination — facilitated mixed-track activity imagining a student in a LARC priority sector
  • Leadership Photo (timing TBD)
  • Closing Call to Action

The formal convening concludes at 2:00 PM. Networking space remains available until 3:30 PM for those wishing to continue conversations informally.

05Common Questions

Before you arrive.

If your question isn't here, reach out to Ruth — she'll get you what you need.

Do I need to RSVP separately for the Kick-Off?

No separate RSVP form is needed. You may have already received — or will soon receive — an Outlook calendar invitation from Ruth Amanuel, the project manager. Please check your inbox and accept the invite; that serves as your attendance confirmation. If the invite hasn't arrived by mid-May, reach out to Ruth using the contact details below.

How do I check in when I arrive on May 22?

On the day of the event, please complete a short check-in form so we can confirm your arrival, finalize lunch counts, and capture any last-minute updates. Look for a QR code at the registration table, or use this link directly: open the check-in form →. It takes about 30 seconds.

Who should attend from each college?

The convening is designed for enrolled track participants, plus institutional leadership — presidents, VPIs, deans of career education, and CTE coordinators. Each partner college is welcome to send leadership in addition to track faculty.

Will the convening be recorded?

Leadership remarks and the vision moment will be recorded for later sharing with colleges that can't attend in person. Photos will be taken throughout the day; the group and leadership photos are intentional moments on the agenda.

What should I bring?

Just yourself, a notebook or laptop, and a willingness to meet faculty from 17 other colleges. All materials — track guides, conversation cards, the regional Canvas Shell tour link — will be provided on-site and online.

Is there a dress code?

Business casual. Comfortable shoes are recommended for the cross-college mixer activity.

How do I access the project after the Kick-Off?

Your enrollment grants access to the regional Canvas course, which serves as the project hub for all four tracks. You'll be walked through it during the afternoon session.
